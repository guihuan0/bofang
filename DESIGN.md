# PipiMusic iOS design

## Direction

Operate mode for browsing and settings. Browsing uses warm black with copper-orange for active controls. The immersive player follows the light turntable reference with a silver deck, album art on the record, and a pale blue lower surface.

## System

- Background: #151210. Raised surface: #211b18. Primary text: #fff3e7. Secondary text: #c7b2a2. Interactive tint: #f4a36e.
- System typography and aligned page edges. Search, settings, navigation, and playback have distinct positions.
- Cover art comes from the track. The search empty state uses a geometric record rather than placeholder album art.
- The compact player sits above the six existing navigation destinations. Its artwork and song text open the immersive player. The player preserves the synchronized lyric preview, lyric seeking, and queue.
- Mobile controls use 44-point touch targets where available. The layout respects safe areas, and landscape puts transport beside the track view.
