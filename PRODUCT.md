## Platform

ios

## Product Purpose

皮皮音乐 lets listeners find, play, save, and download songs on iPhone and iPad.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The iOS build uses a bundled HTML interface inside WKWebView. It supports search history, charts, favorites, playlists, recent playback, downloads, and synchronized lyrics. The player defaults to the favorites order, supports four playback modes, and remembers playback and download concurrency. Downloads use 33ve.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is 皮皮音乐. The existing app icon remains the app identity.
